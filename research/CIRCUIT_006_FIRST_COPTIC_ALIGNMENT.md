## Important finding
The Coptic unit includes both repentance narration and subsequent interpretive discourse, with explicit ordinal markers for the second and third repentances. This supports distinguishing Sophia's own reported experience from Mary/Peter/Martha's interpretation. It does NOT yet prove specific grammar of first repentance or its phonetic realization.
