# Observer-Relative Interpretation — Circuit 001 Addendum

Status: interpretive framework, not a claim established by the Coptic witness.

The observer is not merely Sophia. Preserve simultaneous, distinct observer positions:

1. Sophia: experiencing subject with state-dependent access and interpretation.
2. Other narrative participants: distinct perspectives, intentions, and information access.
3. Narrator: framing and reporting perspective, not interchangeable with Sophia.
4. Reader/listener: situated in language, time, translation, and embodied context.
5. Researcher/model: analytical observer whose categories can distort what is recorded.

## Relational record
For each proposed observation, record: observer identity or unresolved Hole; vantage/context; available evidence; state; observed event; interpretation; confidence; provenance; and transformations between representations.

Do not collapse observation, interpretation, and underlying event into a single assertion. Preserve incompatible or incomplete observer accounts as such. Never infer to create false continuity.

## Open question
Can repeated repentance passages be shown, from verified original-language evidence, to encode changing observer-reference relations rather than only repeated petition? Test rather than assume.
